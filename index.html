How this review is produced

The rules are deliberately simple and stated rather than learned, because a partner reading an alert is owed a rule they can check. Nothing here is written by hand for any individual partner: the same rules run against every partner's data, and most periods produce no flags at all. Opening a different partner account from the internal bar at the top shows that directly: the same rules run on all five and only one of them trips.
